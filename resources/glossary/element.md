# element

Short for _symbol element_. Every sagittal is built from at least two elements. Three types exist: [shafts](shaft.md), [flags](flag.md), and [accents](accent.md). No sagittal has more than four shafts, three flags, or three accents. Shafts and flags together form the [core](core.md).
