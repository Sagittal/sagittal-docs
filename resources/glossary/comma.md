# comma

The word has two meanings in Sagittal:

1. the general term for any of the small pitch intervals that Sagittal symbols represent;
2. the specific term for one of the seven [size categories](comma-size-categories.md) — the one exactly in the middle, with three on either side.

The category _comma_ runs from 11.730¢ to 33.382¢. See [Comma Size Categories](../../concept-explanations/comma-sizes.md).
