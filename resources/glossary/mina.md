# mina

A precision unit at the [Olympian](olympian.md) level: there are **233 minas per [apotome](apotome.md)** (about 0.5¢ each). The name is short for _schismina_ — a singly mina-accented symbol alters by the tridecimal schismina (≈0.42¢).
