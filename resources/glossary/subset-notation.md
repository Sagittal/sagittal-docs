# subset notation

A non-native-fifth EDO notation that treats the EDO as a subset of a larger EDO and samples that EDO's notation — e.g. 8-EDO as a subset of 24-EDO. Used for EDOs whose own fifth is too badly tuned. Contrast [native-fifth notation](native-fifth-notation.md).
