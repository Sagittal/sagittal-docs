# apotome

The Pythagorean **chromatic semitone**, [-11 7⟩ = 2187:2048 ≈ 113.685¢ — the interval from a nominal to its sharp, equal to a chain of seven fifths octave-reduced. Used generically for the chromatic semitone of any temperament. Pronounced uh-POT-uh-mee; from Greek "the part cut away."
