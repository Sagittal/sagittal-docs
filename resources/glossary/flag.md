# flag

One of three types of symbol [elements](element.md), along with [shafts](shaft.md) and [accents](accent.md). There are eight flag types: the left and right versions of the [barb](barb.md), [arc](arc.md), [scroll](scroll.md), and [boathook](boathook.md). A sagittal is often named by its flags.
