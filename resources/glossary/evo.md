# Evo

One of the two [flavors](flavor.md) of Sagittal, short for "evolutionary." Evo uses single-shaft sagittals combined with the conventional sharp and flat symbols. It is the pedagogical stepping-stone, taught before [Revo](revo.md). Formerly called **Mixed**.
