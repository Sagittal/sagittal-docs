# accent

One of three types of symbol [elements](element.md), along with [shafts](shaft.md) and [flags](flag.md). Also called a **diacritic**.

The [core](core.md) of a sagittal consists of its shafts and usually at least one flag. Everything besides the core is an accent. Accents always represent smaller pitch alterations than shafts and flags, so they are always found to the left of the core, and are graphically smaller. Unlike flags, accents may point in different directions from the flags and from each other. The [Herculean](herculean.md) set introduces the first accents (the acute and grave); [Olympian](olympian.md) introduces the breves.
