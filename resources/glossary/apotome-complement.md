# apotome complement

The sagittal that, added to a given sagittal, sums to an [apotome](apotome.md) (a sharp). Because every 2,3-free ratio has two commas that differ by an apotome, a symbol plus its apotome complement makes a sharp. In [Revo](revo.md), complements must be memorized — there is no simple rule.
