# capture zone

The contiguous, non-overlapping range of interval sizes that a single symbol is used to notate. Within its capture zone a symbol represents whatever [comma](comma.md) falls there, defaulting to its [primary comma](primary-comma.md). See also [secondary comma zone](secondary-comma-zone.md).
