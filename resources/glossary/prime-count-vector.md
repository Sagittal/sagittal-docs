# prime count vector

Also called a _harmonic space coordinate_, _prime exponent vector_, or [monzo](monzo.md). It expresses a rational number by how many of each prime it contains: each term is the exponent of the corresponding prime (2, 3, 5, 7, …). For example, 81/80 = 2⁻⁴ · 3⁴ · 5⁻¹ = \| -4 4 -1 ⟩. Trailing zeros may be dropped.
