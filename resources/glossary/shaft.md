# shaft

One of three types of symbol [elements](element.md), along with [flags](flag.md) and [accents](accent.md). The vertical stroke of a sagittal, indicating direction: `|` points up, `!` points down. A sagittal has one to four shafts; multi-shaft symbols belong to [Revo](revo.md).
