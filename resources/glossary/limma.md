# limma

The Pythagorean **diatonic semitone**, [8 -5⟩ = 256:243 ≈ 90.225¢ — a chain of five fifths downward, octave-reduced. In the size-category scheme it is a **medium semitone**. From Greek "a remnant" — what remains of a whole tone after the [apotome](apotome.md) is cut away.
