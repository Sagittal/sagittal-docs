# Revo

One of the two [flavors](flavor.md) of Sagittal, short for "revolutionary." Revo abandons the conventional sharp and flat symbols, using multi-shaft sagittals for every alteration — one accidental per note. Formerly called **Pure**: "if you can't do it in Pure Sagittal, it doesn't exist."
