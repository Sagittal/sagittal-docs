# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is Sagittal too complicated? There are so many symbols!

There are well over a hundred sagittals, but almost no one uses most of them. A mere handful — the [Spartan set](../concept-explanations/symbol-sets.md) — covers something like 90% of what people actually do, and you will not meet the rarest symbols until you go looking for extreme precision. Learn the common ones and use the rest as a dictionary: there when you need a word, ignorable when you don't.

## Do I have to invent my own notation?

Almost certainly not. Sagittal has defined **standard notations** for the popular tunings, and one of them will very likely suit you. Start at [Choosing a Notation](../notation-how-to-guides/choosing-a-notation.md). Rolling your own is a last resort — and if you get there, the [forum](https://forum.sagittal.org) will help.

## Which flavor should I use — Evo or Revo?

Whichever you prefer — they are equally valid and equally endorsed. [Evo](../concept-explanations/evo-v.-revo.md) keeps the conventional sharps and flats, so it is the gentler on-ramp; [Revo](../concept-explanations/evo-v.-revo.md) replaces them with multi-shaft symbols for a cleaner page. Just don't mix the two in one score.

## I'm coming from Johnston or HEJI. Where do I start?

With [Prime Factor notation](../notation-how-to-guides/prime-factor.md) — it is the Sagittal that most closely resembles those systems, one sagittal per prime factor. See also [Sagittal v. other notation systems](../concept-explanations/sagittal-v.-other-notation-systems.md).

## How do I type sagittals without a music font? How do I say them out loud?

Type them as [Sagitype](../concept-explanations/sagitype.md) ASCII (or use [WinCompose](../tools/wincompose.md) to enter the real glyphs), and pronounce them with [Sagispeak](../concept-explanations/sagispeak.md). To write them by hand, see [Sagiscript](../concept-explanations/sagiscript.md).

## Why replace the sharp and flat at all?

You don't have to — that is what Evo is for. Revo replaces them only to gain **one accidental per note** and an unambiguous total direction. The conventional sharp and flat were, in the words of the mythology, "haphazard relics"; Sagittal offers an alternative, but never forces it.

## What does a sagittal actually *mean* — what pitch is it?

A sagittal alters pitch by a **comma**, and its default value is its [primary comma](what-the-symbols-really-represent.md). The exact alteration can depend on the tuning and even on the nominal it sits on. Implementers should read [What the Symbols Really Represent](what-the-symbols-really-represent.md).

## Do I need to learn the commas to use Sagittal?

No. The first thing to learn is which direction the symbol for each prime points — 5 and 7 down, 11 up — and you can read and write a great deal without ever naming a comma. The [comma names](../concept-explanations/comma-names.md) are there when you want the deeper structure.

## Where can I get help or ask something not covered here?

The [Sagittal forum](https://forum.sagittal.org) is the place — the system's creators and community are active there.
