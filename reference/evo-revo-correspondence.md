This is the full **[Evo ↔ Revo](../concept-explanations/evo-v.-revo.md)** (formerly Mixed ↔ Pure) correspondence across one **[apotome](../concept-explanations/apotome-complements.md)** — every standard symbol from the natural (0¢) up to the sharp (113.685¢), in both flavors, from `sheet/07-pure-vs-mixed-apotome-anatomy.csv`.

**How to read the two zones (the "anatomy" of the apotome):**

- **Lower half (0 – ~57¢):** Evo and Revo are *identical* — the same single- or multi-shaft sagittal, no conventional sharp. The Comma column names the [size category](size-category-bounds.md) each symbol falls in (schismina → schisma → kleisma → comma → S-diesis → M-diesis → L-diesis), which is the apotome's anatomy.
- **Upper half (~57 – 113.685¢):** Evo writes a conventional **♯** plus a *downward* single-shaft symbol (the `…#` forms below); Revo replaces that pair with a single **multi-shaft** symbol. Every upper-half symbol is the [apotome complement](../concept-explanations/apotome-complements.md) of a lower-half symbol, mirrored about the half-apotome.
