# Prime Mapping & EDO Consistency

This page will cover how an EDO distorts harmonic space by mapping each prime to an inexact step count, why one sagittal can notate steps of very different-sized EDOs (harmonic function, not cents), and consistency (Paul Hahn's concept, a property of the naive round-to-nearest map).
