# Sagittal-compatible symbols

Not every symbol on a Sagittal score is a sagittal. The [Evo flavor](evo-v.-revo.md) deliberately keeps the conventional sharp and flat, and a few other traditional accidentals are chosen to sit alongside sagittals without clashing. We call these the **Sagittal-compatible symbols**: not arrows themselves, but designed or selected to share the page with them.

## The conventional accidentals

Evo notation uses the ordinary **sharp**, **flat**, **double flat**, and **double sharp** exactly as you already know them — a single-shaft sagittal is written to the left of one of these when needed. <!-- TODO: symbol images -->

One fine point of style: of the two common shapes for the double sharp, only the **large variant** (SMuFL `U+E47D`) is considered stylistically compatible with sagittals — its heavier weight matches the boldness of the arrowheads. <!-- TODO: symbol image -->

## Stein–Zimmermann accidentals and Evo-SZ

The familiar quartertone accidentals — the **Stein semisharp** and the **reversed (Zimmermann) flats** — are Sagittal-compatible too, and they get their own sub-flavor. In **Evo-SZ**, used with tempered systems, any sagittal that alters by exactly half a sharp or flat is replaced by the corresponding Stein–Zimmermann symbol, and the compound cases follow suit. <!-- TODO: symbol images -->

Where the fractional flats are needed, the **narrow** variants (SMuFL `U+E284` and `U+E285`) are preferred, for two Sagittal reasons: they preserve the principle that a symbol's **visual size should track the size of its alteration**, and their narrowness reduces left–right confusion.

{% hint style="info" %}
Evo-SZ exists because the Stein–Zimmermann half-sharps and half-flats are deeply **entrenched** — for something like 72-EDO they are far more compact and familiar than the equivalent sagittal, so it is often kinder to the reader to use them.
{% endhint %}

## Why compatibility matters

Sagittal was designed from the start to *cooperate* with existing notation rather than sweep it aside — the [Evo](evo-v.-revo.md) flavor is exactly that cooperation. Keeping a small set of compatible conventional symbols is what lets a musician who already reads sharps and flats step into Sagittal one comma at a time.
