# Accents

This page will cover the diacritics that add precision beyond about 2¢ — the tick (Herculean), the wing/bird (Olympian), and the tina diacritics (Magrathean) — with the counts of minas and tinas per apotome, and why knowing when to *drop* an unnecessary accent matters more than adding one. (Sources: STV.16; tina/mina email digests.)
