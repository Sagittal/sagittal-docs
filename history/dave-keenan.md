# Dave Keenan

**Dave Keenan** is an Australian engineer and tuning theorist based in Brisbane, and the co-creator of Sagittal. In 2001 he and Paul Erlich derived **Miracle temperament** — the rediscovery that eventually drew George Secor onto the tuning lists — and Dave went on to propose the name **secor** for its generator.

Across an eighteen-year email collaboration with Secor he systematized the notation: refining the symbol sets, running the arithmetic, and building the comma-naming scheme that underlies Sagittal today. He remains the authority on the system's design rationale and history, and maintains [sagittal.org](https://sagittal.org) and the [Sagittal Forum](http://forum.sagittal.org).
