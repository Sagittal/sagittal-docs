# The Sagittal Mythology

Sagittal arrived wrapped in a tongue-in-cheek origin myth. When the Sagittal website opened in 2004, it came with an unfinished story called **Gift of the Gods**, narrated by the Greek god **Hermes** — who claimed to have been impersonating "George Secor" all along. Its conceit is that the notation was handed down by a revived Mount Olympus as a present to the microtonal community, and that every design decision was a divine one.

The myth doubles as a mnemonic, because each symbol is dedicated to a god. **Apollo** fires a golden arrow to decree the single-shaft arrow as the 11-diesis; **Zeus** takes the apotome (a lightning bolt) and **Hera** the natural (which cancels); **Artemis** cleaves the arrow to make the 5-comma flag and is later thanked with the 55-comma; **Dionysus**, **Athene**, and **Ares** receive further dieses. Behind each dedication sits a real principle — that symbols are rational commas, that they invert by mirroring rather than rotation, that straight flags are barbs and curved ones arcs.

The joke had a sequel in real life. In October 2007 the actual George Secor turned up at a microtonal concert and cheerfully claimed to know nothing of the tuning groups or of Sagittal, briefly leaving the community unsure who was who. The full tale is worth reading in [*Gift of the Gods*](https://sagittal.org/gift/GiftOfTheGods.htm).
